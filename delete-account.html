Delete Your Account

Smart Transport · CNB Integrated Logistics Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

Last updated: November 2026

Drivers using the Smart Transport app can request deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time. Follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to developer@cnbtek.com from your registered contact, or contact your fleet/operations manager at CNB directly.
  2. Include your name and the phone number registered on your Smart Transport account, and state that you are requesting account deletion.
  3. We will verify your request and confirm it back to you within 7 business days.
  4. Your account and associated personal data will be fully deleted within 30 days of a confirmed request.

What gets deleted, and what's retained

DataOutcome
Name, phone number, driver profileDeleted
App login/session data, device tokensDeleted
Location historyDeleted
Completed job and EIR document recordsRetained for up to 1 year for operational, billing, and regulatory record-keeping purposes, then deleted

Job and EIR records are tied to completed logistics transactions involving third parties (ports, customers) and are kept for a limited period to meet standard business and compliance record-keeping obligations, even after your personal account is deleted.
