Privacy Policy

Smart Transport · CNB Integrated Logistics Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

Last updated: November 2026

Smart Transport ("the App") is developed and operated by CNB Integrated Logistics Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("CNB", "we", "us") for use by our drivers to manage container pickup, delivery, and documentation during trips. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Who this app is for

Smart Transport is an internal operational tool intended solely for use by CNB's employed and contracted drivers. It is not intended for use by the general public and is not directed at children.

2. Information we collect

Location data: We collect precise (GPS) location, including in the background while a trip is active, to show your route to pickup, track trip progress, and confirm arrival at pickup/drop locations.

Camera and photos: The App uses your device camera to scan Equipment Interchange Receipt (EIR) documents. Scanned images and the data extracted from them (container numbers, types, sizes, ISO codes, port/CFS details) are uploaded and stored against the relevant job.

Personal and account information: Your name, phone number, assigned vehicle number, and driver profile details are used to identify you within the App and assign jobs to you.

Microphone: The App requests microphone access as part of a bundled device/document-capture library. We do not use audio recording for any App feature.

Bluetooth: Used only to connect to paired peripheral hardware (e.g. printers or scanning accessories) where applicable.

Device and usage data: Basic technical data (device type, OS version, crash logs, network status) is collected to keep the App stable and functioning offline when network connectivity is poor.

3. How we use your information

All data collected by the App is used strictly to operate core App functionality: assigning and tracking jobs, confirming pickup/delivery, processing EIR documents, and keeping your trip data in sync. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to any third party.

4. Data sharing

Your data is not shared with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Data may be shared with CNB's own backend infrastructure providers solely to operate the service (e.g. cloud hosting), under contractual confidentiality obligations.

5. Data security

Data is transmitted between the App and our servers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Access to stored data is restricted to authorized CNB personnel.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained for as long as you are an active driver with CNB, and for a reasonable period afterward for operational and legal record-keeping. To request deletion of your personal data, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's privacy

This App is not directed at, and must not be used by, children. It is strictly an internal tool for employed/contracted adult drivers.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this same location with an updated "Last updated" date.
